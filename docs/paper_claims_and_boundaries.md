# Paper Claims and Boundaries

## 1. Recommended Working Title

**From Plausibility to Mechanism: Counterfactual Error-Invariant Distractor Generation for STEM Multiple-Choice Questions**

---

## 2. Main Claim

> We propose a no-reference, distribution-free framework for evaluating and generating STEM distractors through mechanistic validity, counterfactual error invariance, and minimax diagnostic separability.

---

## 3. Contributions

1. **Mechanistic Distractor Validity**：将 distractor quality 从学生分布拟合重新定义为可执行错误机制证据。
2. **Mechanism Metrics**：提出 MEV、CEI、MDS、MDV。
3. **Error-Trace-First DG**：通过 correct trace → error operator → wrong trace → formal verifier 生成 distractors。
4. **Leakage-safe Protocol**：严格隔离 mechanism_only 与 human_seeded_proxy。
5. **Expert-aligned Evaluation**：验证 MDV 与专家 mechanism faithfulness / pedagogical usefulness 相关。

---

## 4. Claims We Can Make in MVP

- The proposed system produces distractors with higher executable wrong trace rates than prompt-only baselines.
- The proposed metrics evaluate distractors without relying on original distractors or student response distributions.
- CEI captures whether an error mechanism remains stable under construct-preserving transformations.
- MDV is correlated with expert ratings of mechanism faithfulness and pedagogical usefulness.
- Mechanism-only DG is a stronger basis for STEM construct validity than LLM-as-student-proxy simulation.

---

## 5. Claims We Should Not Make Yet

- The method estimates real student choice probabilities.
- The method replaces human pilot testing.
- The 50-item MVP proves cross-domain generalization.
- Student Choice Prediction is fully reproduced without student data.
- LLM respondent panels provide valid psychometric response distributions.
- MDV is an IRT/CTT parameter.

---

## 6. Suggested Introduction Thesis

Current distractor generation systems often optimize surface plausibility or approximate student response distributions. However, in STEM domains, a distractor is educationally meaningful only when it can be traced to a specific, local, executable error mechanism. We therefore shift the target of DG from plausibility alignment to mechanism invariance: a distractor should be generated by a typed error operator, verified through a wrong reasoning trace, and remain structurally stable under construct-preserving counterfactual transformations.

---

## 7. Suggested Limitation Paragraph

This work evaluates mechanism-level and construct-level validity rather than population-level psychometric functioning. Although the proposed MDV score is designed to be no-reference and distribution-free, it does not estimate empirical student choice probabilities. Human pilot studies and larger-scale response data are still required to establish item discrimination, distractor functioning, and calibration across learner populations.
